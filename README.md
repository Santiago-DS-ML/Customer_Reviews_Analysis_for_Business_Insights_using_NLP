# 📊 Customer Reviews Analysis for Business Insights using NLP

## 🧠 Project Overview

In today’s data-driven environment, companies collect large volumes of customer feedback but often struggle to extract actionable insights.

This project aims to analyze customer reviews using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques in order to identify key drivers of customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction, and translate them into business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer sentiments (positive, negative, neutral)
- Identify recurring themes in customer feedback
- Detect key pain points and strengths
- Provide actionable business insights

---

## 📂 Dataset

- Source: Amazon customer reviews dataset
- Data type: Textual customer feedback
- Sample size: Subset of reviews used for analysis

---

## ⚙️ Methodology

The analysis follows a simple and interpretable NLP pipeline:

1. **Data Cleaning**
   - Lowercasing
   - Removing punctuation and noise

2. **Sentiment Analysis**
   - Classification into positive / negative / neutral

3. **Keyword Extraction**
   - Identification of frequently used words

4. **Theme Identification**
   - Grouping keywords into business-relevant categories:
     - Product quality
     - User experience
     - Brand perception
     - Technical issues (screen, battery)

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- ✅ **88.4% of reviews are positive**, indicating strong overall customer satisfaction
- ⚠️ Negative feedback highlights recurring issues related to:
  - Screen durability
  - Battery performance
- 📱 Products such as phones and accessories are most frequently discussed
- 💬 Customer experience is generally positive but impacted by specific technical issues

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Strong product perception is a key strength to maintain
- Technical issues represent critical improvement areas
- Customer satisfaction is high but sensitive to product reliability

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

1. **Improve product reliability**
   - Focus on screen and battery performance

2. **Segment product categories**
   - Identify high-risk product groups (phones, accessories)

3. **Monitor customer feedback**
   - Track negative keywords over time

4. **Enhance product communication**
   - Align customer expectations with product capabilities

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

This project demonstrates how NLP can transform unstructured text data into actionable business insights.

Rather than focusing on complex models, the emphasis is placed on interpretability and business value.
